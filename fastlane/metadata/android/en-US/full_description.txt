Most budgeting apps give you a spreadsheet. Dayzo gives you one number.

Enter your monthly income once. Dayzo divides it across the days of the month and shows what you can spend today. Log a spend and watch the number drop. Green means you are fine, amber means slow down, red means you are over.

What you get
- A daily budget that adapts to the length of each month
- Two-tap spend logging with quick-add chips for common purchases
- Today's budget, amount spent, and a progress bar that changes color as you use it up
- Month pulse: are you ahead of or behind your plan this month
- A streak of consecutive days under budget
- A 14-day history chart and a category breakdown
- Optional morning reminder with your number and a night check-in with today's summary
- Indian rupee formatting (1,00,000)

Private by design
Everything stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no servers. Delete the app and your data is gone.

Dayzo is a one-time purchase. No subscriptions.
