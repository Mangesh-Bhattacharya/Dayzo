Questions


How is the daily number calculated?

Monthly income divided by the number of days in the current month, rounded down.

Does it read my bank or UPI messages?

No. You log spends yourself, which takes two taps. That is also why it needs no permissions to read your messages or accounts.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. There is no account and no server.

No. It is a one-time purchase.
